# Multi-Platform Video Distribution Architecture

One-click publishing engine supporting 9 vertical short platforms:

1. **YouTube Shorts** (`#Shorts` API metadata)
2. **TikTok** (Trending audio & caption tags)
3. **Instagram Reels** (Aspect ratio & cover optimization)
4. **Facebook Reels** (Cross-posting feed sync)
5. **LinkedIn Video** (Professional video feed)
6. **Pinterest Idea Pins** (Visual pin cards)
7. **Threads** (Short video post sync)
8. **Snapchat Spotlight** (Spotlight submission)
9. **X Video** (Inline vertical video stream)
