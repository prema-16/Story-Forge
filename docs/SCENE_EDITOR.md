# Scene Editor & AI Copilot Tools Documentation

## Overview
Allows per-scene property customization and one-click AI copilot editing actions.

## AI Copilot Operations
- **Extend Scene**: Generates 5s AI visual continuation.
- **Shorten Scene**: Trims dead air & silence gaps.
- **B-Roll Suggestions**: AI recommends visual B-roll clips based on scene script.
- **Auto Silence Removal**: Cuts audio gaps > 0.5s automatically.
- **Auto Subtitle Sync**: Aligns SRT timestamps to voice duration.
